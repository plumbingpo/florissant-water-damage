Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Florissant

A sump pump has one job, and it tends to fail at the moment it is needed most. Heavy rain raises the water table, the pit fills, and then the power goes out, the float sticks or the pump cannot keep up. Water rises over the rim of the pit and spreads across the basement floor. It keeps coming until a pump is running again.

Do not think of it as clean water. Soil, silt, bacteria and the contents of the drain tile travel with it, so our team rates it no better than Category 2, and Category 3 when it has stood for a while or sewage is involved. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Before We Arrive

  1. Keep Out of the BasementDo not step into a flooded basement while the power is on. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. If the panel is in the water, call the utility.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceSee if the plug pulled out, the outlet has tripped or the float got stuck on the side of the pit. Keep your hands out of the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyChildren and pets stay on the upper floor, anyone who has to enter wears rubber boots and gloves, and hands get washed after contact with anything wet.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallTake pictures of the pump and the water level, bring valuables that are still dry upstairs, and phone us. Waiting for it to recede without help is a mistake.
Coverage

Insurance and Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

This is one of the most misunderstood claims. A standard homeowners policy usually excludes water that backs up or overflows from a sump, and flood insurance is a separate policy with its own rules. Unless you bought an endorsement for sump overflow or water backup there is often no coverage, and such endorsements are capped. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. To keep the claim file complete, our team documents the damage, the failed pump and the height of the water.

Our Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Process

1

Get the Water Out

We bring our own pumps and extractors, so nothing depends on your failed sump. Standing water and silt are removed from the floor, the pit area and any low spots.

2

Remove Contaminated Materials

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Clean and Sanitize

The slab, wall framing and remaining contents are washed down and treated with an antimicrobial product. This happens before drying so contaminants are not blown around the basement.

4

Dry and Verify

Typically for 3 to 5 days, air movers and dehumidifiers stay in place, until the concrete and framing read normal on a moisture meter. Make sure a functioning pump is installed again by the time drying wraps up.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • Stuck or snagged float switches, which never tell the pump to start.
  • Loss of power at the pump alone, from a loose cord or a tripped circuit.
  • Undersized equipment that falls behind when inflow is heavy.
  • Losing electricity in a storm when the pump has no battery backup.
  • Water returning down the discharge pipe because the check valve no longer seals.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Basement Mechanical Equipment

Mechanical equipment often shares the floor with the sump pit. If wiring, burners or control boards got wet they may be ruined, and no appliance should be restarted until it has been checked.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

The wet zone inside a wall reaches well past the water mark, as drywall and insulation pull moisture up off the floor. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Wall Framing

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Carpet and Flooring

Groundwater-soaked carpet and padding come out, as cleaning cannot make them safe. Silty water also gets caught below vinyl plank and laminate, which normally means lifting them.
